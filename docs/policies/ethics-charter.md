# Ethics charter

Post-Mine Sentinel watches places where some of South Africa's most vulnerable people work in deadly conditions, often trafficked or coerced. We therefore commit to the following:

1. **Safety first, not manhunts.** An alert triggers *safe egress and shaft sealing*. Nobody is pursued underground. Mine Rescue Services lead any underground response. (Lesson: CRD Mine, July 2026.)
2. **Land, not people.** We sense ground vibration and warmth, not faces, voices, phones or identities. We will not add facial recognition, voice capture or phone tracking.
3. **A human in the loop.** An alert is a reason to inspect, never proof of a crime. Every enforcement decision is taken and recorded by an accountable person.
4. **Need to know.** Locations of active sites and sensors are restricted, generalised for low-clearance users and audited, so the platform can never become a map for syndicates.
5. **Honest models.** We publish how models were trained (synthetic data), how they fail (domain shift) and their measured error rates. We do not overstate accuracy in pitches.
6. **Proportionality.** Monitoring is limited to registered mine sites and their immediate surrounds. No general surveillance of settlements.
7. **Accountability.** Alerts are signed; actions are audited in an append-only log; auditors can verify everything independently.
8. **Community engagement.** Before a site is instrumented, the local municipality and community structures are informed of the safety purpose and of the dangers of entering shafts.
9. **Humanitarian referral.** Responses include referral pathways (human-trafficking response, health care, labour support) for people brought to the surface.
