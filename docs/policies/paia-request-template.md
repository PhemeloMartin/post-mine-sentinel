# Template: PAIA request for derelict and ownerless mine data

*Use the prescribed form (Form 2 under the PAIA Regulations) and attach this schedule. Check the current form, fees and the information officer's details on the department's PAIA manual before submitting.*

**To:** Information Officer, Department of Mineral and Petroleum Resources (and/or the Council for Geoscience)
**From:** [Name, organisation, contact details]
**Date:** [ ]

## Records requested
1. The current **Derelict and Ownerless Mines database** (all fields), including site identifiers, coordinates, commodity, hazard classification (e.g. "high-risk", "asbestos"), openings/holings, and rehabilitation status and dates.
2. **SAMINDABA** (South African Mineral Deposits Database) extracts for the same sites: commodity, production history and closure dates.
3. The rehabilitation prioritisation methodology and the annual rehabilitation plans for 2020–2026.
4. Records of reported illegal-mining incidents at D&O sites, aggregated per site and year (no personal information).

## Format
Machine-readable (CSV, GeoPackage or Shapefile) with a data dictionary.

## Purpose (public interest)
To support research and a proposed public-safety early-warning and prioritisation system for derelict mines, addressing findings of the Auditor-General regarding database completeness and accuracy and rehabilitation prioritisation. No personal information is requested.

## Undertakings
The information will be stored in South Africa and secured under POPIA s19, used only for the stated purpose, and not published at a precision that could aid illegal mining.
