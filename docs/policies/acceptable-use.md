# Acceptable use policy

By signing in to Post-Mine Sentinel you agree that:

1. You use the system only for your role's purpose: mine-safety monitoring, rehabilitation planning, enforcement of mining law, or oversight.
2. You do **not** share, screenshot, export or discuss precise site or sensor locations outside authorised channels.
3. You do **not** use the system to track, profile or identify individuals.
4. You keep your credentials and MFA device private and report suspected compromise immediately.
5. You understand that all access is logged in a tamper-evident audit trail and reviewed.
6. You report any defect that could cause a missed alert or a false alert.

Violations may lead to revocation of access, disciplinary action and, where applicable, criminal charges (Cybercrimes Act 19 of 2020; POPIA offences).
